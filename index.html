How Does An Edible Cup Work?

A seamless, delicious ritual that replaces single-use waste with pure delight in four simple steps.

1

Pour Your Drink

Pour steaming hot Indian chai, fresh espresso, cappuccino, or cold ice creams and puddings straight into the cup.

2

Sip at Your Pace

Enjoy your drink leisurely. Our food-grade natural barrier keeps the cup 100% leak-proof and crunchy for 40+ minutes.

3

Crunch & Eat It!

When your drink is finished, don't toss a disposable cup into the trash—eat it! It tastes like a delicious, freshly baked cookie biscuit.

4

100% Zero Waste

Zero plastic, zero microplastics in hot beverages, zero landfill pollution. Even if discarded, it naturally biodegrades in days.

The Hard Truth

Why "Paper Cups" Are Not Eco-Friendly

Billions of so-called paper cups end up in landfills every year. Here is why the world urgently needs Cup2Eat.

Traditional "Paper" Cups

The Plastic Deception
  • Hidden Plastic Lining: Coated inside with polyethylene (PE) or wax plastic, making them virtually impossible to recycle.
  • Microplastics in Hot Tea: Lab studies show boiling tea releases over 25,000 microplastic particles into your beverage in 15 minutes.
  • 30+ Years in Landfills: Takes decades to decompose, choking drains, soil, and municipal waste systems.
  • Creates Tons of Trash: Every tapri and cafe generates dozens of black trash bags of soggy cup garbage daily.

Cup2Eat Edible Solution

Eat Your Drink Revolution
  • 100% Edible & Natural: Baked from natural food grains, vegetable extracts, and real cocoa/cardamom with zero synthetic additives.
  • Zero Microplastics: Clean, pure, FSSAI certified food-grade composition safe for children, adults, and families.
  • 40+ Mins Leak Resistance: Engineered to hold piping hot chai without getting soggy until you are done.
  • Zero Landfill Footprint: Eat the cup as a snack! Even if dropped, it's 100% biodegradable organic food waste.
